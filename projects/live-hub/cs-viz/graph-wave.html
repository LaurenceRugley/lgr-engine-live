Why it matters: BFS finds the fewest-edges path to every node — that's why it's the tool for shortest paths on unweighted graphs. DFS goes deep first — the natural shape for "explore a maze," topological sort, and cycle detection. Both visit every node exactly once, so both are O(V + E); they differ only in the order, and that order comes entirely from queue-vs-stack. — part of the Mission Control curriculum.
