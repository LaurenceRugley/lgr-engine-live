Sort Race

The same shuffled array, sorted three ways at once. Insertion sort is O(n²); merge and quicksort are O(n log n). Grow the array and watch the comparison counters diverge — that gap is the difference between the complexity classes. Press Step to advance one operation each, or Play to run.

Reading it: comparisons (blue) are the fair currency of a sort — each is one "is a > b?". At n = 32, insertion averages roughly n²/4 ≈ 256 comparisons; merge and quick land near n·log₂n ≈ 160. Small here — but double the array and insertion quadruples while the others barely move. Moves (pink) are writes/swaps: insertion shifts, merge copies into a scratch buffer, quicksort swaps in place. — part of the Mission Control curriculum.
