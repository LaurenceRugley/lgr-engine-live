Engine showcase

The Open City

A living procedural city: fly a craft over it, sweep day↔night, roll weather & seasons, switch style tiers (beauty / toon / pixel / vector), and apply colour-grade looks. Dive into an office interior, or flip on survival mode in-city.

Controls: drag / two-finger to orbit · scroll / pinch to zoom · use the on-screen control bar for day-night, weather, tiers & looks.

Survival game

The Hoard

A top-down wave-survival game showcasing the arena stack: flow-field horde AI, projectile ballistics, GPU particle FX, blood & scorch decals, and a rigged zombie horde. Press F to DIVE into first person and walk the forest arena in the thick of the horde.

Controls: WASD / left stick move · mouse / drag aim · hold click / FIRE to shoot · F = dive to first-person (then WASD walk + mouse-look) · I = bag & crafting.
Play the Hoard → Forest arena (rigged horde)
One-shot · staged

Hoard v2

The Hoard rebuilt fresh in a single multi-agent run on the same engine: a decrepit forest-and-ruins arena, three distinct zombie types crowd-pathing a flow field, build-and-breach fortification, harvest + hunger/stamina survival, a real day↔night cycle with a harder night, and the iso→first-person dive. Staged for playtest — rough edges and all.

Controls: WASD / arrows move · mouse aim · hold click to fire · Space / V melee · F = dive to first-person · I = bag & crafting.
