Algorithms, alive

Zero-dependency CS visualizations — no WebGL, instant load, works on any phone.

Sort Race → Event Loop Graph Wave

Every chip is a URL. The rooms read their dials from the query string, so a setup you like is a link you can keep or hand to someone. Inside the World Lab and the Swing Lab, the dock's copy url button writes the current dials — world and physics — back out as one.

The World Lab's physics are live. rope, gravity, assist and maxSpeed take effect on the next frame with no rebuild; the terrain dials (seed, grade, street, cols, spacing, woods) regenerate the island. With no parameters at all, every room is exactly its shipped build.
