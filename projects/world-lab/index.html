WORLD LAB — THE PATCHWORK

TERRAIN + CARVE (rebuilds the world)
DISTRICTS (the coverage dial)
VERBS
ONE world, five CONTIGUOUS districts — city, woods, desert, lakes and the surrounding sea. Every texel belongs to exactly one; each district's largest connected blob holds ~100% of it (per-texel biome classification cannot: that is speckle, not a place); and the coverage slider means what it says — ask 26% woods, count 26% off the finished field. District height offsets fade to ZERO at every seam, so nothing the region system adds is a cliff. Inside it all, the city still sits ON the terrain: pads flat, streets ramped, towers authored off their OWN pad. One world bag serves walking, the bike and the swing.
